Donate

Give a rupee; get a receipt, a report and the truth.

Every donation is entered in a numbered receipt book the day it arrives, carries an 80G certificate in April, and appears — in total, by head — in the audited accounts we publish whether the year was flattering or not. Last year, 88.4 paise in the rupee reached programme work.

01

What your gift becomes

Restricted gifts are honoured exactly; unrestricted gifts go where the board's budget says.

₹1,100 pays for a complete school kit — bag, slates, notebooks, geometry box — on one child's back for the year.

This site takes no payment online — the methods below are the payment. The choice above simply tells you, and us, what your gift becomes.

Four methods

  • UPI — alhindsociety@upi instant · receipt by SMS
  • Bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS) receipt same day
  • Cheque or demand draft payable at New Delhi
  • At the office counter Mon–Sat, 10–6

After any method, message your name, postal address and PAN (for the 80G certificate) to +91 98714 06340 or alhinddevelopments.society@gmail.com if we don't already have them. A numbered receipt follows the same day; the certificate follows in April.

Drawn in favour of Al Hind Educations & Developments Society, payable at New Delhi. Write your phone number on the reverse; the receipt book entry quotes the cheque number.

At the counter

Cash and cheque accepted at House No. 14A, Sethi Enclave, Phase 1, D.K. Mohan Garden, West Delhi, Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 6 pm. The receipt is issued before you leave the counter. Ask to see the receipt book itself — it is on the counter for exactly that reason.

Tax exemption (80G)

The society holds registrations under sections 12A and 80G of the Income-tax Act. Donors resident in India may claim the deduction applicable to them; the 80G certificate for all gifts in a financial year is posted in April. Keep your receipts; the numbers on them match the certificate.

A standing promise, monthly

The society's steadiest money is a small monthly promise — ₹300, ₹500, ₹1,000 — set up as a recurring UPI mandate in your own bank app, in your own control, cancellable in two taps. Write to us and we will send the year's plan so your mandate has a destination for each month.

Goods, and what we can use

New blankets, school material, steel bottles and treadle machines are always welcome; call before sending anything else. Second-hand clothing, with respect, we cannot store or sort — the godown is small and the need is specific.

Foreign contributions

The society holds no FCRA registration and therefore cannot accept foreign contributions, including from NRIs through foreign accounts. We say this plainly rather than discover it in an audit. Domestic gifts from NRIs through Indian accounts are, of course, welcome.

03

What we promise you in return

A numbered receipt the same day; an 80G certificate in April; the annual report by post if you want paper, by mail if you don't; and an open invitation — visit any centre, any working day, unannounced. If a year's accounts slip below what we promise on this page, you will read about it in the report before you read about it anywhere else.

The society will never call you to ask for more. Fundraising by telephone is a programme we have declined to run, on principle, since 2014.
