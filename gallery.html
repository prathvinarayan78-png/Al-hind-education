Gallery

Scenes, not poses.

The society is pre-launch, so nothing here is a record — every frame is a representative scene of how the work will look, Insha'Allah. And the rule is fixed before the first photograph: beneficiaries are never photographed, not for donors, not for the press, not for this website. The gallery shows welfare from the other side of the table: boards, benches, kits, pumps, machines and registers.

On consent: staff photographs are taken with the subject's say over the frame; villagers at a distance appear only where the frame's owner agreed. Requests to withdraw a frame are honoured within a week, no questions asked.

Al Hind Educations & Developments Society

Education and on-the-ground public welfare across Delhi's urban villages and bastis. Registered 2014; now raising Nasih Public School. Accounts audited and published from the first year of operations — and measuring what matters: readers, not enrolments.
