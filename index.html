Where exactly are you in Mohan Garden?

A-Block, Main Mohan Garden Road, 2 mins walk from Nawada Metro Pillar 742, opposite City Hospital. Look for green Al-Hind board. Uttam Nagar Terminal is 1.5 km away — E-rickshaw ₹10.

What are the fees? Any installment option?

Class 9-10 from ₹1,499/mo, 11-12 from ₹1,999/mo, NEET/JEE from ₹2,499/mo. Pay monthly, 3-month or yearly (extra 10% off). Sibling & girl-child discounts available at Mohan Garden branch.

Do you have transport or online classes?

We are a walkable neighbourhood centre — most students from Mohan Garden, Vikas Nagar & Nawada walk/cycle. For days you miss, get App access with recorded lectures + PDF notes same day. No hybrid fee extra.

Is there a hostel/PG nearby for outstation students?

We help you find verified PGs in Mohan Garden & Uttam Nagar (₹5k-7k/month with food). Separate PG list for girls with guardian contact.
