A Message From The Chief Executive

“Education is not a business.
It’s an amanah.”

When we registered Al-Hind Education Society in 2014, Mohan Garden had talent, but no nearby guidance. Parents travelled to Mukherjee Nagar or Laxmi Nagar for coaching. I asked — why shouldn’t our children get the same quality, here, at home?

Naim Bilal Nadvi built Al-Hind on three principles that still drive every class in Mohan Garden:

Niyyat First

Teach for impact, not just marks. Character before career.

Small Batches

Max 40. Teacher knows every name, every weak chapter.

Affordable

Installments, sibling discounts, scholarships — no child left behind.

“In 11 years, 3,000 students have passed through these doors — from 62% to 96%, from shy to fluent, from Mohan Garden to AIIMS, DU, Jamia. The greatest trophy is when a former student returns as a teacher.”

Naim Bilal Nadvi
Chief Executive, Al-Hind Education Society (Regd. 2014)

This new scroll-driven website is his next step — making Al-Hind’s experience as modern as its teaching. Every scroll you make mirrors a student’s journey: small steps, big transformation.
