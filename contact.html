Contact & visits

A person answers, not a menu.

The office keeps ordinary hours and an ordinary phone. For centre visits, unannounced is fine — the registers and the bulbs are the same every day — but if you want a coordinator's company, give us a day's notice. Tuesdays are cluster days everywhere; the best day to watch the society think.

03

Before you visit a centre

Three courtesies: the class is the children's hour, so visitors sit at the back and take calls outside; the registers are open to read but are the village's paper, so photographs of them need the coordinator's nod; and if you come bearing sweets or gifts, hand them to the teacher for later — a child who is watched receiving is a child performing, and we don't run performances.

Panchayat members inviting a centre should bring the gram sabha resolution or the school's letter, and the name of the space offered. The baseline survey follows within a month of the June training cycle.
